Tactical Combat Guide
  • Spearmen (🔱) beat Knights (🐎) (3x damage).
  • Knights (🐎) beat Archers (🏹) & **Catapults (💥)**.
  • Archers (🏹) beat Spearmen (🔱) (2x damage).
  • Catapults (💥) beat **Castles/Citadels** (4x siege damage).
  • Champions (🛡️) beat Spearmen, acting as standard tanks.
